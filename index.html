05Questions
Will customers know it's AI?+

Mostly no. The voice is natural, it interrupts and recovers like a person, and it never runs a phone-tree menu. We disclose it where the law requires, and it costs nothing in bookings.

What if it doesn't know the answer?+

It says so, takes a message, or warm-transfers the call to a human on the spot. It never invents an answer and never quotes a price you didn't approve.

Do I need a new number?+

No. We forward your existing line, or set the agent to pick up only when your team doesn't. Your number, your caller ID, nothing to reprint.

What does it plug into?+

Google Calendar, Calendly, GoHighLevel, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, HubSpot, and anything else with an API. Bookings, notes and transcripts land where your team already works.

How much of my time does setup take?+

One thirty-minute call and about an hour of testing before launch. We write the scripts, build the integrations and tune the voice. You're live in a week.

Can I cancel?+

Month to month, thirty days' notice. Your number forwards back the same day and you keep every recording and transcript.
